Positive and safer communication

Communicate with Open PALMS™

A simple, memorable model that helps people communicate positively while maintaining personal safety.

Open hands. Palms visible, posture calm, intention clear.

Why it matters

What we communicate without words can shape what happens next.

Open hands with palms facing upwards are generally perceived as positive and non-threatening. Combined with thoughtful positioning, space and attention, they can support calmer interactions.

The model

Five principles. One safer approach.

Select each principle to explore the practical behaviour behind it.

01 / Position

Keep the environment working for everyone.

Think about where you are standing and what is around you. Ensure both you and the person you are communicating with have a clear exit path.

  • Avoid blocking doorways
  • Notice obstacles and pinch points
  • Choose a position that supports choice

02 / Attitude

Let helpful intent show.

Adopt a positive, respectful and helpful approach. Your tone, expression and pace can all influence how your message is received.

  • Use a calm, even tone
  • Stay curious rather than judgemental
  • Focus on what can be done next

03 / Look & Listen

Make attention visible.

Use appropriate eye contact and show that you are listening. Give the person time to speak and check your understanding.

  • Use culturally appropriate eye contact
  • Notice changes in voice and body language
  • Reflect back key points

04 / Make Space

Distance can reduce pressure.

Maintain an appropriate distance for the situation, taking account of intimate, social and stranger space.

  • Avoid crowding
  • Adjust for the setting and individual
  • Create room to move safely

05 / Stance

Stand in a way that feels less confrontational.

Position yourself slightly to one side rather than square-on. Keep your posture balanced, your hands visible and your movement measured.

  • Use a relaxed, natural posture
  • Keep palms visible where appropriate
  • Avoid looming or sudden movement
